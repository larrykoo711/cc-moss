认识一下小莫Meet Mo

住在桌面一角的小伙伴A little friend in the corner of your desktop

平时安安静静，会眨眼、会看你的鼠标。有会话等你了，它先冒个泡；你凑过去，它才把话说完。愿意的话，它还能叮一声，或者直接说出来。Quiet most of the time; it blinks and follows your pointer. When a session needs you, it gives a little puff first and only says the rest when you come over. If you like, it can chime, or say it out loud.
